Prod-owner feedback: my access-control scan was partly stale by the time it was read. Confirmed against current prod:

- `anon` on `tokens` is **SELECT only** — I mischaracterized this as a write hole. The `PATCH tokens` 204 I saw was a false positive of my own test method (see below).
- **No anon/patient writes exist anywhere** — the PATCH/DELETE 204s reported for `tokens`, `counter_services`, `audit_log`, `appointments`, `doctors`, `organizations`, etc. were an artifact of my test design: a PATCH/DELETE filtered on a UUID that matches zero rows returns `204` whenever the role holds the *table-level* grant, regardless of whether RLS would actually block it on a real row — and I called that out as a caveat in the report, but still led the summary table with "any 2xx on a write" language that read as a confirmed write hole. It wasn't. Sorry for the noise.
- `counter_services` **has RLS** — confirmed wrong in my report.
