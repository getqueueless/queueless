## Top P0/P1 for the owners (read this first)

1. **Live status pages don't actually update live** — the core "leave the line" promise is broken in prod right now (#1).
2. **Printed walk-in slip QR/link points at `localhost:3000`, not `lpu.lol`** — every real patient's slip is dead on arrival (#2).
3. **`/admin/staff` is hard-broken** with a leaked env-var error, and it's why every counter shows "Unnamed staff" (#3).
4. **Staff/admin login always lands on the patient profile gate**, not `/admin` or `/counter` (#4).
5. **Patient's own `/my` page promises three actions and has zero buttons** (#5).

## Bugs

### P0 — blocks demo

---
**#1 — Patient status page and TV board never update without a manual reload**
Route: `/t/<id>` and `/display/<service_id>`
Owner: **api** (realtime wiring) + web-patient / web-admin (consumers)

Steps:
1. Issue a token at `/kiosk` (Pediatrics), open its `/t/<id>` link in one tab.
2. Open `/display/<pediatrics-service-id>` (the waiting-room TV board) in another tab.
3. As counter staff on the matching counter (`/counter`), click **Call Next** until that token is called, then **Done**.
4. Do NOT reload the two tabs from step 1–2. Just look at them.

Expected: both pages say, in their own words, "Updates automatically. No need to refresh." — the status should flip from Waiting → Called → Being served → Done live, and the TV board's counter tile should flip to the new token, all without a reload.

Actual: both pages sit frozen on stale state (`WAITING` / "You're next", and the TV tile stays blank) for 20+ seconds after the token was actually called and served. A manual reload immediately shows the correct, current state — so the data layer is fine, only the push/subscription to already-open tabs is broken.

Screenshots: [17-token-status-live-update.png](screenshots/17-token-status-live-update.png) (stale, right after call), [19-token-status-stale-check.png](screenshots/19-token-status-stale-check.png) (still stale 14s+ later), [23-token-status-after-manual-reload.png](screenshots/23-token-status-after-manual-reload.png) (correct after reload) · [20-tvboard-liveupdate-check.png](screenshots/20-tvboard-liveupdate-check.png) (stale board) vs [22-tvboard-after-reload-data.png](screenshots/22-tvboard-after-reload-data.png) (correct after reload)

**#2 — Kiosk-printed slip QR code and link point at `localhost:3000`, not the production domain**
Route: `/kiosk` (after issuing a token)
Owner: **web-admin** (almost certainly a base-URL env var pointing at localhost in the prod build)

Steps:
1. Sign in as counter staff, go to `/kiosk`, pick any service, click **Get token**.
2. Look at the printed slip preview's QR code and the link printed under it.

Expected: `https://lpu.lol/t/<id>` — what a patient's phone can actually reach.

Actual: prints `http://localhost:3000/t/<id>`. Confirmed via page text, not just visually. Every walk-in patient handed a real slip today gets a QR/link that fails on their phone — this breaks the entire product concept for anyone who took a token at the desk instead of typing the link by hand.

**#3 — `/admin/staff` 500s with a leaked internal error; breaks staff management and counter names**
Route: `/admin/staff`
Owner: **api** (missing `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` on the prod deploy)

Steps: Sign in as admin, go to **Staff** in the sidebar.

Expected: a list of staff/admin accounts, or at worst a generic "couldn't load" message.

Actual: `GET /api/admin/staff` → `500`, and the page renders the raw server error **"Server is missing SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY."** directly in the UI. Two problems in one: (a) staff management is completely unusable, and (b) an internal implementation/config detail is exposed to the browser, which is explicitly the thing `docs/JUDGE_NOTES.md` says this app avoids ("An unexpected server error never shows a stack trace or internal detail to the caller"). This is also almost certainly why **every counter on `/admin/counters` shows "Unnamed staff"** even though the underlying staff→counter assignment clearly works (counter1/2 log in and land on their correct, named desks).

Screenshots: [05-admin-staff-500-bug.png](screenshots/05-admin-staff-500-bug.png), [admin-counters.png](screenshots/admin-counters.png) (all "Unnamed staff")

---
**#12 — Web app is missing `Content-Security-Policy` and `Strict-Transport-Security` headers that the API already sends**
Route: all of `lpu.lol` (checked via response headers on `/`)
Owner: **web-admin** / infra

`api.lpu.lol` sends `content-security-policy: default-src 'none'` and `strict-transport-security: max-age=63072000; includeSubDomains`. `lpu.lol` (the Next.js frontend) sends neither, though it does send `x-content-type-options`, `x-frame-options`, `referrer-policy`, and `permissions-policy`. Also leaks `x-powered-by: Next.js` (trivial, but free to strip).

## What's clean (verified, not just assumed)

- **Accessibility:** axe-core 4.9.1, 0 violations on `/`, `/staff?tab=password`, and `/kiosk`.
- **Performance:** homepage TTFB ~404ms, full load ~421ms, ~10KB document transfer — good for the demo network.
- **Error states:** invalid/expired token link (`/t/<bogus-id>`), wrong OTP code, and empty profile-form submit all show clear, friendly, on-brand messages with no console errors.
- **OTP resend cooldown works:** requesting a second code immediately for the same email correctly throttles with "Couldn't send the code. Try again in a moment."
- **RBAC:** counter staff hitting `/admin` directly is correctly bounced back to `/counter`, not shown the dashboard.
- **Admin Services / Counters / Priority settings** pages: no console errors, no visual issues, forms and tables render correctly, dark and light theme both checked.
- Full call flow (Call Next → Done, three times in a row) worked correctly end-to-end once verified via reload — the queue engine itself, no-show/priority config, and kiosk token issuance are all solid.

## Not tested (explicit — see why)

- **Full OTP email round-trip** — no readable test inbox provided; only the UI states (send/wrong-code/cooldown) were tested, per instructions.
- **Queue-full state** — services have a 500/day cap; not practical to drive to that number against production without disrupting real demo data.
- **True offline/network-drop state** — no reliable network-throttle available in this environment; attempted via request interception and abandoned rather than leave the session in a broken state.
- **Doctor screens** — prod has 0 doctors (no seed data yet, per your note). Blocked, not a bug.
- **Google OAuth sign-in** — needs a real Google account; out of scope for this pass.
- **Payments** — explicitly out of scope; never touched with real or test cards.
